# Saferry Data Sources — v14

Last reviewed: 2026-10-06

## 1. Ferry schedules — primary source
Saferry now treats the **Super Shuttle Roro operator schedule** as the primary source for the Super Shuttle Ferry Hagnaya ↔ Santa Fe route:

- https://www.supershuttleroro.com/schedules

The operator page currently lists daily Hagnaya → Santa Fe departures at 2:00 AM, 4:00 AM, 7:00 AM, 8:00 AM, 10:30 AM, 12:30 PM, 2:30 PM, and 4:30 PM; and Santa Fe → Hagnaya departures at 4:00 AM, 8:30 AM, 10:30 AM, 12:30 PM, 2:30 PM, 4:30 PM, and 6:30 PM.

### Cross-check sources
These are used for comparison only and are **not silently merged** into the operator schedule because published times differ:

- BARKOTA / SuperShuttleFerry: https://www.barkota.com/super-shuttle-ferry/
- Pamasahe.com — Super Shuttle Ferry 2026: https://pamasahe.com/routes/hagnaya-santa-fe-super-shuttle-ferry-schedule-fares
- SYKE Travel & Tours 2026 ferry reference: https://bantayanisland.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Ferry-Schedule-2026-All-In-One_V2.pdf

### Schedule data policy
Ferry schedules can change or be cancelled because of weather, port/tide conditions, safety restrictions, maintenance, or operator decisions. Saferry presents the selected source as **reference information**, not a guarantee of a sailing. Users should confirm the latest sailing with the operator/port before travel.

## 3. Safety guidance sources

Sea-travel guidance is based primarily on Philippine Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA) passenger-safety guidance:

- https://marina.gov.ph/2025/10/30/paalala-sa-mga-pasahero-ng-barko-at-bangka-ngayong-undas-2025/
- https://marina.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MARINA-Advisory-No.2026-04.pdf
- https://marina.gov.ph/2026/09/23/panoorin-ano-nga-ba-ang-no-ticket-no-boarding-policy/

Beach-safety guidance is based on:

- Iloilo City CDRRMO / Iloilo Coast Guard beachgoer reminders: https://cdrrmo.iloilocity.gov.ph/resources/disaster-preparedness/pahanumdom-sa-mga-beachgoers/
- Philippine sanitation rules for natural bathing places (Supreme Court E-Library): https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/42156

## 4. Source policy
Saferry should prefer **official operator and government sources** for operational and emergency information. Secondary sites may be used as cross-checks, but conflicting schedules must not be silently combined. Every dataset should carry a last-checked date and source URL.
